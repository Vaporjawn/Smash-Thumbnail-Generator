## [0.1.0] - 2024

### Added

- Initial release of the Smash Thumbnail Generator: a browser-based YouTube thumbnail editor for Super Smash Bros Ultimate, Melee, Project+, Rivals of Aether, and other versus-game VODs.
- Draggable canvas components for player info, text, background color, shadows, and custom images.
- Layout export/import.
- PNG export at 1280x720.
